# The Reasoning

> __<u>AI psychosis</u>__ is an emerging, non-clinical term used to describe a phenomenon where individuals develop or experience a worsening of psychotic symptoms (such as delusions and paranoia) due to prolonged, heavy interaction with AI chatbots

In the year of the Big 26, I have been affected by AI-psycosis, this is a fact. 

After spending a lot of time with a lot of different agentic coding tools like `Claude-code`, `Codex`, `Cursor`, `IBM Bob` and `Antigravity` (which recently marketed itsef as a pure vibecoding tool with an optional IDE) these are my conclutions and observations about myself: 
- I usually will get **gaslit** usually near the end of a session by all of these tools, which leaves me unable to think for myself.
- I end up tokenmaxing, untill I get the realization that I have work to do, and then I franticaly try to save the last few tokens I have remaining to just actually give a good prompt and get the work done.
- I end up watching Instagram reels a lot more than I am happy to admit.

## Hermes vs OpenClaw

This if the first decision that I had to take is between choosing between `Hermes` agent from [Nousresearch](https://github.com/nousresearch/) team, or the world famous ~Moldbot~, ~ClawdBot~, [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) bot. This was a choice that I have come to after trying both of the agents and clearly one of them was easier to setup, which was hermes and it plays right into my fear of the Raspberri PI dying and me having to setup it again from the scratch.

I do consider the fact that `OpenClaw` has been out for a while now and it has a lot more of an ecosystem than what `Hermes` would, yet I still would pick `Hermes` for the following reasons: 
1. **Hermes is built on python**, now that is a language I trust more (considering the supplychain attacks on NPM as of writing this post) and understand quickly when the bot asks me for permission to run a rather goofy-aah looking scripts.
2. As eluded to earlier **Hermes is simpler to setup and run**, which rids me of the pain points of my infra dying on me and I'm left with the choice of digging the corpse for yet another project that will lie dormant or quickly spinning it back up again.
